Oistars Billings provides enterprise-grade financial management, invoice lifecycle tracking, recurring subscription billing, and merchant settlement payouts tailored for European businesses and modern digital merchants.

Engineered with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Oistars Billings delivers an offline-first, sovereign accounting experience backed by an encrypted local Room SQLite database with optional cloud sync and telemetry.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Invoicing & Receivables Management
• Generate, issue, and reconcile professional invoices with configurable payment terms.
• Full European VAT compliance support with customizable tax rates (0%, 9%, 21%).
• Instant PDF generation with automated digital signature hashing (SHA-256) and shareable receipts.
• Filter and track invoices across all states: Draft, Pending, Paid, and Overdue.

2. Recurring Subscriptions & MRR Analytics
• Manage subscription plans across weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual billing cadences.
• Track core financial metrics: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), Churn Rate, and Active Subscriber counts.
• Automated billing interval calculations with next-due-date projections and renewal statuses.

3. Merchant Settlement Payouts & AML Protection
• Disburse settled merchant balances via SEPA Direct Bank Transfers and express payouts.
• Automated compliance validation with anti-money laundering (AML) and sanctions blacklist verification.
• Real-time ledger accounting: track available balance, pending settlements, cleared reserves, and completed disbursements.

4. Client Store Directory & Relationship Ledger
• Maintain a comprehensive client database with registered addresses, VAT identifiers, and contact details.
• Instant access to per-client lifetime value (LTV), transaction counts, and issued invoice histories.
• Rapid customer lookup with real-time multi-field search and sorting.

5. Sovereign Data Security & Privacy
• Offline-first architecture: core financial records are stored securely on-device using Android Room SQLite.
• Strict Google Play Data Safety compliance: zero personal data selling, zero behavioral advertising trackers, and transparent data deletion controls.
• Production error tracking and network latency observability powered by Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring.

6. Open Source & Verifiable
• Open source under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
• Built with Material Design 3 and adaptive layout support for smartphones, foldables, and tablets.
